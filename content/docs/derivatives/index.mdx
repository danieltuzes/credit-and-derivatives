<PartBridge>

Part 1 gave present value, no-arbitrage replication, and risk-neutral pricing
as a discounted expectation over a finite set of states. This part applies
those results to the first derivative contracts. Replication fixes the forward
delivery price and put-call parity, and the risk-neutral expectation values an
option, first on a one-period tree and then node by node on a multi-period
lattice.

</PartBridge>

<PartScope>

This part covers forwards and European options on one underlying. The first
chapter separates a forward's obligation, its fair delivery price, and the
value of an existing contract. The second chapter separates an option's right
and expiry payoff from its current value, premium, and profit. The third
chapter matches call and put portfolios to derive put-call parity. The last
two chapters value an option: on a one-period binomial tree by replication and
by risk-neutral weights, and on a multi-period lattice by backward induction
with local discount factors.

</PartScope>
