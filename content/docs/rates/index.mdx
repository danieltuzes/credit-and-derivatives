<PartBridge>

Part 1 defined a discount factor for one future date from a rate quote, and
Part 2 applied one discount factor to each promised bond payment. This part
collects the discount factors of many dates into one deterministic discount
curve. The credit-curve chapter of Part 7 uses that curve as the risk-free
discount input of a CDS.

</PartBridge>

<PartScope>

This part has one chapter. It reads a deterministic discount curve that is
input at a set of dates, interpolates log-linearly between those dates, and
calculates the forward discount factor between two future dates as the ratio
of their discount factors from valuation time. The curve is an input: the
chapter does not calibrate it to market prices, and it does not extrapolate
beyond the last input date.

</PartScope>
