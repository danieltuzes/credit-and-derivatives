One basis point is one hundredth of one percentage point.

$$
1\,\mathrm{bp}=0.01\%=0.0001.
$$

The unit describes a rate difference. A move from $4.10\%$ to $4.35\%$ is
$25$ basis points, not $0.25$ basis points.
