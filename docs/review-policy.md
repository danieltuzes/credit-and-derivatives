# Review policy

All content begins as `draft`. Move it to `in-review` only when its sources,
assumptions, competencies, assessment evidence, and numerical checks are ready.
Move it to `reviewed` only after separate editorial and quantitative passes.

A reviewer must verify the source itself rather than relying on an AI summary,
search snippet, or citation title. Any change to a formula, convention,
calculator, answer key, contractual statement, workflow, dependency, or policy
requires focused human review.

Changing reviewed content makes it draft again unless the reviewer explicitly
re-approves the changed scope.
