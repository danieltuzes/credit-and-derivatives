# Market conventions

No convention should be hidden inside a calculator default. Lessons and labs
must expose applicable valuation and settlement dates, calendars, business-day
adjustments, day counts, compounding, payment frequency, clean/dirty treatment,
recovery, default timing, accrued-on-default, and quotation conventions.

Every contractual or market-practice convention needs a registered source and
an effective or access date. A model assumption must be labeled as an
assumption rather than presented as market fact.
