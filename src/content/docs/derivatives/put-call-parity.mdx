## What you will be able to do

After this lesson, you should be able to:

- show that long call plus short put has a forward-style expiry payoff;
- state the exact contract terms that must match;
- solve a missing European call or put value using discounted strike and known income.

## Match the expiry cash flows

For the same \term\{derivative-underlying-value\},
\term\{option-strike-price\} $K$, and \term\{option-expiry-time\}
$T_{\mathrm{opt}}$, the holder payoffs satisfy the state-by-state identity

$$
\max\!\left(S_{T_{\mathrm{opt}}}-K,0\right)
-\max\!\left(K-S_{T_{\mathrm{opt}}},0\right)
=S_{T_{\mathrm{opt}}}-K.
$$

The left side is long call plus short put. The right side is the payoff of a
long forward-style exchange at strike $K$. This is an identity in every expiry
state, not a forecast or an average.

## Present-value parity

Let local \term\{parity-income-present-value\} be $I_0$. The local
\term\{prepaid-forward-value\} is
$\widetilde{F}_{0,T_{\mathrm{opt}}}=S_0-I_0$. Applying the law of one price to
the matched portfolios, with \term\{call-option-value\} $c_0$ and
\term\{put-option-value\} $p_0$, gives

$$
c_0-p_0
=\widetilde{F}_{0,T_{\mathrm{opt}}}
-K D(0,T_{\mathrm{opt}}).
$$

Because

$$
\widetilde{F}_{0,T_{\mathrm{opt}}}
=D(0,T_{\mathrm{opt}})F_{0,T_{\mathrm{opt}}},
$$

the same relation can be written in forward form:

Here $F_{0,T_{\mathrm{opt}}}$ is the matching \term\{forward-price\} for option
expiry, not the fixed delivery price of an older forward.

$$
c_0-p_0
=D(0,T_{\mathrm{opt}})
\left(F_{0,T_{\mathrm{opt}}}-K\right).
$$

Hull derives put-call parity by comparing the expiry values of two portfolios
and invoking no arbitrage\cite\{hull-options-futures\}\{Ch. 10 §10.4, printed
pp. 221-225, European put-call parity and arbitrage portfolios\}.

For $c_0=8$, $\widetilde{F}_{0,T_{\mathrm{opt}}}=97$, $K=100$, and
$D(0,T_{\mathrm{opt}})=0.95$,

$$
p_0=8-97+100(0.95)=6.
$$

## Where parity stops

The equality needs the same underlying, strike, expiry, exercise style,
quantity, income treatment, and settlement basis. American exercise changes
the timing choices. A default-triggered knock-out changes the default-state
cash flows: if both options terminate on issuer default, call-minus-put is also
zero in that state. Its matching forward-style claim must therefore terminate
on the same event; the vanilla formula cannot be copied unchanged.
