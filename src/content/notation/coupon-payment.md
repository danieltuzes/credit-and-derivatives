The level coupon payment $C$ is the periodic cash amount promised by the toy
fixed-rate bond.

Its definition nests \term{face-value}, \term{annual-coupon-rate}, and
\term{bond-payment-frequency}:

$$
C=\frac{cF}{m_B}.
$$
