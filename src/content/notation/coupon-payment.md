The level coupon payment $C$ is the periodic cash amount promised by the toy
fixed-rate bond.

Its definition nests [[face-value]], [[annual-coupon-rate]], and
[[bond-payment-frequency]]:

$$
C=\frac{cF}{m_{\mathrm B}}.
$$
